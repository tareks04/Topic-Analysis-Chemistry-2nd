Scope: The attached PDF contains six pages: a cover and topic-history tables for five chapters (PDF pages 2–6). The Chapter 1 table is clipped at the bottom of page 2. It is not the complete textbook; chapter totals are not printed for independent reconciliation.
  • Counting rule: each year label inside an M year list is counted as one listed entry; repeated labels are preserved.
  • Classification basis: M (medical) lists only. D (dental) lists are retained in each topic’s details and in the JSON export.
  • Missing M data: Several rows with D-only lists have no M group, and one Chapter 1 row is clipped with its topic name unreadable. These records are unknown/unverified, not zero.
  • No explicit zeros: no topic row in these five tables explicitly prints a zero-question count.
  • Text quality: topic labels are Bengali transcriptions from scanned images; names have not been translated. Review the scan if exact orthography is critical.
  • Interpretation limit: this source supports frequency of listed year entries, not guaranteed unique question counts or predictions of future exam topics.
